## 1. TOP — Russia's missiles land in Poland (war in Europe)

- Overnight, Russia fired more than 400 drones and 110 missiles at Ukraine in one
  of its heaviest barrages of the war; at least eight people were killed in Kyiv
  and 28 injured (AP / The Times).
- A Kh-101 cruise missile came down near Nowy Lipowiec in south-east Poland
  around 6:30 a.m. local time, about 50 km from the Ukrainian border; a second
  suspected missile fell near Rudnik nad Sanem and a jet-powered drone near
  Molodycz. No casualties or damage reported in Poland.
- Polish PM Donald Tusk: "an intentional provocation" — the objects fell close
  to where allied "coalition of the willing" troops were holding exercises.
  NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte called it a "grave violation" of Polish
  airspace; NATO jets were scrambled.
- Sources: AP (via europeansays), BBC, The Times, The Guardian, The Insight
  International (published 2026-11-21T01:10Z).

## 4. Houthi missiles intercepted over Riyadh

- Sources: Reuters (21 Nov), Al Jazeera, TBS News.

## 5. Cyclone Akvan fades over Odisha, India

- Cyclone Akvan, which made landfall near Gopalpur on Friday, weakened on
  Saturday into a low-pressure area over north interior Odisha, the India
  Meteorological Department said.
- More than 300,000 people had been evacuated to 9,341 shelters; Ganjam
  district recorded extremely heavy rain (Patrapur 209 mm, Hinjilicut 207 mm).
  No major damage or loss of life reported so far; heavy rain and flash-flood
  risk continue in Odisha, West Bengal, Jharkhand and Bihar.
- Sources: Sambad English (published 21 Nov 09:19 IST), ITDC India
  (21 Nov 10:08 IST), The Daily Jagran.
